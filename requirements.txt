Why do RNNs struggle with long-term dependencies?
RNNs can forget earlier words when sequences become very long.

How do LSTMs help?
LSTMs contain memory cells that help remember important information for longer periods.

What does attention solve?
Attention helps the model focus on the most important words in a sentence instead of treating all words equally.

Why are transformers important?
Transformers process text faster and understand context better. Modern AI systems like ChatGPT use transformer architecture.
